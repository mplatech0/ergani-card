Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how My App handles your personal information when you use our application and its integration with Google Drive services.

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate using your Google Account, we request access to your Google Drive via Google OAuth 2.0. The access permissions (scopes) granted are strictly limited to what is necessary for the app to perform its functions (e.g., saving and loading application data).

2. How We Use Google Drive Data

Our app accesses your Google Drive solely to:

  • Read and write application-specific data/files that you explicitly authorize.
  • Ensure seamless data synchronization across your sessions.

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your Google Drive data to any third parties. We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or training machine learning models.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your Google Drive files remain stored within your personal Google account infrastructure. Our application processes your data locally or directly through secure encrypted API connections to Google APIs. We do not store your private Google credentials or Drive content on external databases.

4. Limited Use Disclosure

My App's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Service User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. User Rights & Revoking Access

You can revoke our application's access to your Google Drive at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions Settings.
