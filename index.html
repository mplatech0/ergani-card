A simple and secure application integrated with Google Drive.

About the Application

Our app helps you seamlessly manage and sync your data using your personal Google Drive storage. By integrating directly with Google Drive API, we ensure that your data stays safe, secure, and fully under your control.

Key Features

  • Secure authentication via Google OAuth 2.0.
  • Direct integration with Google Drive for data backup and synchronization.
  • No intermediate storage of your personal Google Drive files on external servers.
